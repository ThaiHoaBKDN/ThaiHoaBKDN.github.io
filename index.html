About Me

I received the Mechatronics Engineering degree from The University of Danang - University of Science and Technology in 2023. I am currently a graduate student at Chungnam National University, under the supervision of Professor Wonkeun Youn.

My research interests include linear control, nonlinear control, extended state observers, autonomous underwater vehicles, and autonomous surface vehicles.

  • Nonlinear control
